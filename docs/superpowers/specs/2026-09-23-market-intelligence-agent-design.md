# Superseded design record

This earlier design described a broader Market Intelligence Agent product and is no longer the product specification. The accepted product scope is the Windows Enterprise Research Workbench documented in [PRD.md](../../PRD.md), [architecture.md](../../architecture.md), and [release-gates.md](../../release-gates.md).

The desktop framework and Pi Agent foundation from Folio remain. Securities, quotes, portfolios, screening, investment theses, Longbridge, and related finance capabilities are out of scope. Enterprise research is limited to public information; the documented 6 tasks, 3 strategies, and 24 fixture cases are the active evaluation boundary. No Craft Agents source is claimed.
